WWII Visual Identity & Theme Guide
Color Palette:

Background: Aged Parchment (#EFE8D8) or Dark Munitions Slate (#24272A)

Military Olive: #3D493A (Headers, structural cards)

Blood & Stamp Red: #8B2626 (Highlight boxes, dates, badges)

Brass/Khaki: #B89C62 (Subheadings, borders)

Primary Text: Charcoal Black (#1C1E20) or Warm White (#F5F5F0)

Typography:

Titles: Oswald, Bebas Neue, or Special Elite (Vintage Typewriter / Stencil look)

Body: Georgia, Roboto Slab, or Courier Prime (Clean, legible historical print)

Visual Motifs: Muted sepia filters on archival photos, classified stamp badges (RESTRICTED / ARCHIVE), topographic map textures, and cardboard paperclip frames.
